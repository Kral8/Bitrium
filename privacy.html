Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 5, 2026

Bitrium Network we respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains how we handle your data within our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Account Data: Name, Email address, and Birth year provided during registration to personalize your mining experience.

Device Data: Device model, OS version, and unique advertising identifiers (IDFA/AAID).

Mining Statistics: Mining session start times, active speeds (mining_speed), and ad-boost history.

2. Cloud Mining Simulation

Bitrium Network is a Cloud Mining Simulation. We do not use your device's hardware (CPU/GPU) to perform actual mining. All calculations and balance accruals are handled on our secure cloud servers.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app integrates third-party SDKs that may collect information to provide ads and analytics. These include:

  • Google Firebase: Used for authentication and real-time database management.
  • Appodeal: Our primary ad mediation platform.
  • Ad Networks: Unity Ads, AppLovin, and IronSource.

4. How We Use Data

We use collected data to operate the mining simulator, track your referral network, provide rewarded video bonuses, and prevent fraudulent activity. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

5. Security

We use industry-standard encryption and Firebase security protocols to protect your balance and profile data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
